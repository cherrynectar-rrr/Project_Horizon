# Japan MSc Feasibility Audit v1

Last Updated: 2026-08-28
Owner: Voyage Room
Status: Strategic Research / Advisory
Decision Authority: Project Control

## 1. Purpose

Evaluate Japan as a serious 2029 MSc route for Project Horizon rather than as a casual backup.

The audit is centered on the user's verified context and current Horizon constraints:

- normal four-year undergraduate graduation in 2029;
- Electronic Information Engineering background;
- long-term interest region includes robotics, control, intelligent systems, embedded systems and AI deployment, without locking one final identity;
- GPA, mathematical/ECE foundations and real project/research evidence remain important;
- current overseas MSc affordability planning band is approximately RMB 100,000/year all-in;
- TUM ECE → Automation and Robotics remains the current Germany benchmark;
- TOEFL is the preferred common English examination;
- GRE remains conditional;
- no simultaneous activation of several high-intensity language/test lines without a Control decision.

This file does **not** activate a Japan route or Japanese-language study. It establishes a verified strategic alternative for later comparison with Germany and other destinations.

## 2. Executive Judgment

Japan should be upgraded from a generic alternative to a **formal high-potential strategic candidate**.

The strongest current reason is not merely cost. Japan contains high-level national-university English-taught master's routes in engineering that can align closely with EIE → control / robotics / intelligent systems while avoiding Japanese as an admission prerequisite.

The most important current finding is:

> **Institute of Science Tokyo International Graduate Program (C), especially Systems and Control Engineering / Electrical and Electronic Engineering, is an unusually strong Horizon fit and should become the primary Japan benchmark for subsequent lab-level research.**

University of Tokyo IME, particularly Electrical Engineering and Information Systems, is a strong reach option but currently makes GRE directly relevant because GRE General is required by that program.

Tohoku IMAC-G is a strong robotics option but is structurally more mechanical/mechatronics-oriented. University of Osaka has promising English EEE/mechanical routes. Kyushu ISEE Global Course is very ECE-aligned but currently retains formal oral/written examination structure and requires a deeper procedure audit. Nagoya G30 offers useful English options but the cleanest current match is narrower. Kyoto remains academically excellent but its most obvious English master's route found in this audit is Energy Science rather than a clean English ECE/control route.

## 3. Financial Feasibility

### Verified current baseline

Japan's official Study in Japan portal currently reports approximate first-year graduate-school academic fees of:

- National university: JPY 820,000
- Local public university: JPY 900,000
- Private university: JPY 1,100,000 (excluding medicine/dentistry/pharmacy)

Official estimated average living cost for international students is approximately JPY 105,000/month excluding academic/research costs. Housing varies substantially by region, with Tokyo above the national average.

Science Tokyo currently lists:

- application fee: JPY 30,000;
- enrollment fee: JPY 282,000;
- annual tuition: JPY 635,400.

Many other national universities in the current pool list around JPY 535,800/year tuition plus JPY 282,000 enrollment fee, though fees can change.

### Planning interpretation

Under current exchange rates and cost data, a self-funded national-university master's in Japan is **plausibly compatible with Horizon's approximately RMB 100,000/year planning band**, especially outside the most expensive Tokyo housing scenarios.

Do not treat current FX or current tuition as a 2029 guarantee. Reprice the full route in 2028 after target-pool freeze.

### Scholarship upside: MEXT

Current 2027 MEXT Research Student rules provide, for regular master's/professional degree students:

- JPY 144,000/month allowance;
- waiver of university entrance examination, matriculation and tuition fees under the scholarship rules;
- economy-class transportation to Japan and return transportation under specified conditions.

MEXT is therefore a major upside scenario, but **must not be used as the base affordability assumption**.

Embassy Recommendation is also a substantial independent application process: document review, Japanese and English written language tests, interview, research plan, provisional university acceptance and final placement. A candidate can be placed initially as a non-regular research student or directly into a regular degree depending on the acceptance route and university decision.

## 4. The Japan Admissions Model

Japan should not be modeled as “Germany with different forms.”

A recurring pattern across the current Japanese routes is:

1. identify a research field / laboratory;
2. contact or obtain consent from a prospective academic supervisor;
3. submit transcript, research/study plan and recommendations;
4. pass programme-specific document screening, interview and/or subject examination;
5. enter the master's degree directly or, in some scholarship/research-student routes, begin as a research student before a later degree-course examination.

This means the user's future **research question quality, project/research evidence, ability to explain technical work, and professor/lab fit** have greater operational importance than in a purely centralized admissions model.

This is strategically compatible with Horizon's emphasis on verified projects, research exposure and course evidence.

## 5. University / Programme Audit

### Tier J1 — Primary Japan benchmark

#### Institute of Science Tokyo — International Graduate Program (C)

Current verified facts:

- full master's degree available;
- spring and fall intake;
- lectures and seminars in English;
- no Japanese-language admission requirement under current IGP(C) rules;
- 18 participating departments;
- relevant participating master's departments include Systems and Control Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Information and Communications Engineering, Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering and others;
- prospective supervisor consent is required before the application can proceed;
- admission includes interview and/or examination; English ability is evaluated during admission;
- external English test score is no longer a universal IGP application requirement from spring 2025 onward, although a supervisor or scholarship application may request one;
- current fee baseline: JPY 30,000 application, JPY 282,000 enrollment, JPY 635,400/year tuition.

Strategic fit:

**Very High.** Systems and Control Engineering is a direct match to the control / robotics branch, while EEE provides a natural EIE continuation. The English-only degree architecture removes the need to acquire Japanese solely for admission.

Main future audit:

- identify 8–15 laboratories across Systems and Control / EEE / robotics-adjacent groups;
- map their research to the user's future real coursework/projects;
- inspect current selection questions and whether individual supervisors expect TOEFL despite the programme-wide rule;
- recheck 2029 intake fees and procedure.

Current Horizon position: **Japan benchmark / high-priority future lab audit.**

## 6. Language Strategy

### Admission language and career language must be separated

A key finding is that Japan has English-only graduate admission routes in engineering where Japanese is not an admission requirement.

Therefore, **Japanese should not be activated now merely because Japan is strategically interesting.**

Current recommendation:

- continue English foundation → TOEFL architecture because it serves Germany/multi-school applications and many Japanese routes;
- do not add JLPT/N1/N2 to the current application-gates workload;
- if Japan later becomes the leading destination, introduce Japanese as a living/career integration line based on evidence and capacity rather than treating N1 as an automatic admission prerequisite.

### Employment reality

English-only degree ≠ English-only career in Japan.

JASSO's 2027 international-student job guide reports that Japanese companies place substantial weight on Japanese communication. In the cited survey, around 69% of science-field employers sought intermediate-business-or-higher Japanese at the job-offer stage and around 86% sought that level after joining; Japanese-language skill was also one of the most commonly cited qualities sought in international students.

Japan's new-graduate hiring system also starts early: official Study in Japan guidance describes job hunting beginning around March of the year before final graduation, meaning a two-year master's student may need to start career preparation during the first master's year.

Strategic consequence:

- English route is feasible for **admission**;
- if long-term goal becomes employment and life in Japan, Japanese would become a meaningful future career investment;
- this is a reason to delay language activation until Japan's route probability rises, not a reason to start German + Japanese simultaneously now.

## 7. Application Evidence Implications for Undergraduate Years

Japan increases the value of several things Horizon already wants:

1. **GPA / transcript quality** — remains a first-order admissions signal and MEXT first-screening input.
2. **Core ECE and mathematics courses** — supports lab fit and programme screening.
3. **Research training / course paper** — unusually valuable because Japanese applications often require a research/study plan and supervisor match.
4. **One or more explainable projects** — helps establish technical maturity and research direction.
5. **Recommendation relationships** — UTokyo IME currently requires recommendations; MEXT requires recommendation documentation.
6. **English technical communication** — useful in interviews and research-plan discussion.
7. **Course evidence preservation** — still valuable for applications and later comparison, though Japan does not use Germany's APS/VPD system.

This means the user's current Year 2 Fall `Electronic Information Engineering Professional Scientific Research Training and Course Paper` deserves special observation: if it provides a real research topic, advisor relationship, literature work or report, it could materially strengthen both Japan and general MSc readiness.

## 8. Germany vs Japan — Current Strategic Comparison

| Dimension | Germany / TUM benchmark | Japan English-track benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admission language burden | German proof currently major for TUM ECE + English may still matter elsewhere | Some top engineering routes require no Japanese for admission; English/technical interview can suffice |
| Admissions structure | curriculum/prerequisite verification, APS/VPD, language proof, programme aptitude | lab/professor match, research plan, document screening, interview and sometimes subject exam |
| Route centralization | relatively programme-centered | often more laboratory/supervisor-centered |
| Financial fit | conditional; TUM tuition materially affects current route | national universities generally strong fit to ~RMB100k/year band under current costs |
| Scholarship upside | programme/country-specific; needs case-by-case | MEXT is a major full-support upside, but competitive and separate |
| Main hidden risk | German time cost, prerequisite matching, future tuition/living changes | professor/lab fit, entrance-exam variance, possible travel, Japanese career language |
| Synergy with current EIE | strong for ECE/control | very strong for Science Tokyo EEE/Systems & Control; strong for several others |
| Career language | German valuable/required for much of German life/job market | Japanese valuable for broad Japanese new-grad market even if degree is English |

### Current Voyage judgment

Japan is **not automatically better than Germany**. The trade is different:

- Germany asks for a long language/curriculum-verification runway.
- Japan can reduce admission-language burden through English tracks, but replaces it with stronger lab/research-plan/supervisor fit and heterogeneous entrance procedures.

The user's future real research/project evidence may therefore be more decisive in Japan-route ranking than it is in the current Germany baseline.

## 9. Recommended 2026–2028 Decision Architecture

### 2026 H2 – 2027

Do:

- preserve Japan as a formal strategic candidate;
- build GPA, ECE/math foundations, Python/Algorithm and real engineering evidence;
- treat the Year 2 research-training course as a possible research-entry gate;
- continue English foundation;
- perform a limited Science Tokyo + UTokyo lab map when enough technical vocabulary exists to evaluate labs meaningfully.

Do **not**:

- start JLPT preparation solely because Japan is now interesting;
- stop German immediately based on one strategic audit;
- activate GRE solely for UTokyo today;
- open a high-volume Japan application-preparation branch three years early.

### 2027–2028

Evidence gate:

- Has the user developed a credible control/robotics/intelligent-systems research preference?
- Is there meaningful professor/lab fit at Science Tokyo / UTokyo / Osaka / Tohoku etc.?
- Is the financial picture still favorable?
- Does the user prefer Germany/Europe or Japan after more real engineering and language experience?

If Japan probability rises materially:

- begin practical Japanese at a bounded level for life/career integration;
- deepen research proposal and lab contact preparation;
- consider MEXT Embassy route timing;
- retain TOEFL;
- activate GRE only if the retained target pool justifies it.

### 2028 H1 Target Pool Freeze

The existing Horizon rule to freeze Target Pool v1 no later than 2028 H1 remains useful.

At that point classify each Japanese programme by:

- exact lab/professor;
- application deadline;
- April/October intake compatibility with June 2029 graduation;

- Japanese requirement;
- in-person exam/travel requirement;
- supervisor-consent deadline;
- research-plan/recommendation requirements;
