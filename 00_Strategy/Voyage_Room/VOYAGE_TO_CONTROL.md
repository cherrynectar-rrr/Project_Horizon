Ordinary non-EU tuition plus Milan living is not safely inside the budget, but current merit scholarships provide full tuition waiver and some include annual allowances.

Self-funding is likely above the strict band, while GSFS currently offers full tuition for up to four semesters plus a stipend for selected international graduate students.

- Institute of Science Tokyo (#=97) — excellent Systems & Control fit, but Tokyo living plus current tuition makes funding important.
- KTH (#=82) — excellent Systems, Control and Robotics academic fit; scholarship can waive tuition but Stockholm living alone consumes most/all of the annual budget, making it a lower-priority scholarship reserve.
- TUM (#25) — excellent curriculum fit remains, but current third-country tuition and Munich living costs fail the baseline affordability test. Keep only as a major-funding / living-stipend moonshot.
