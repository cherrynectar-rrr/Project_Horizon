## Purpose

## Capture Rules

- Record only user-confirmed facts, durable preferences, constraints, concerns or clearly marked open questions.
- Keep facts separate from hypotheses.
- New interests do not automatically become long-term routes.
- When later statements change earlier ones, mark the older interpretation superseded rather than blending contradictions.
- Route-changing conclusions go through `VOYAGE_TO_CONTROL.md`.
- School curricula, competitions, certificates and institutional programs are inputs rather than personal strategy.
- For undergraduate course timing, the user's actual execution-plan screenshots take precedence over conflicting timetable information in the generic training-plan PDF.
