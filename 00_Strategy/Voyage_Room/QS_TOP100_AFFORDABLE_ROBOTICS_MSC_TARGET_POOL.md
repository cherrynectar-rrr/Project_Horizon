# QS Top-100 Affordable Robotics MSc Target Pool

Last Updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Voyage Room
Status: Strategic target-pool hypothesis; 2026 snapshot; advisory only

## Purpose

Build a current MSc target pool under the user's explicit hard filters:

1. university must be in the **QS World University Rankings 2027 overall Top 100**;
2. programme must preserve a credible route from Electronic Information Engineering into robotics / embodied AI / intelligent systems / automation / embedded intelligent systems;
3. realistic annual all-in cost should be approximately **RMB 100,000** after predictable tuition policy and/or verified scholarships/waivers;
4. uncertain part-time work must not be the mechanism that makes an otherwise unaffordable route viable;
5. programme-level curricular fit matters more than prestige alone.

This is a 2026 planning snapshot, not a 2028–2029 admission guarantee. Rankings, tuition, scholarships, living costs, language requirements and admissions rules must be rechecked in the actual application cycle.

## Recommended Primary Pool

### 1. Université Paris-Saclay — #76 — CORE TARGET

**Recommended route:**
- M1 International Track in Electrical Engineering (English)
- then robotics/autonomous-systems-aligned M2 such as Mobile Autonomous Systems, Mechatronics/Machine Vision/AI, or Smart Aerospace and Autonomous Systems, depending final prerequisites and availability.

**Why it fits the user:**
- M1 explicitly accepts Electronics / Electrical Engineering / Automation backgrounds.
- Curriculum combines mechatronics, embedded systems, computer vision, AI, electronics, signal processing and intelligent systems.
- M2 Mobile Autonomous Systems explicitly covers perception–decision–action, control, embedded sensors, signal/image processing and mobile/aerial robotics.
- This is structurally close to Electronic Information Engineering -> autonomous intelligent systems without requiring the undergraduate degree to become pure CS or mechanical engineering.

**Current cost architecture:**
- 2026–2027 national Master registration fee: EUR 255.
- Paris-Saclay currently grants non-EU students subject to differentiated fees a partial exemption bringing them to domestic-rate registration fees under its current policy.
- CVEC is EUR 105 in 2026–2027.
- University/CROUS-linked housing listings currently include roughly EUR 250–538/month rooms/studios, though supply is limited and priority criteria apply.
- International Master's Scholarship: EUR 10,000/year; an M1 award is automatically renewed for M2 if required credits are completed and the student is admitted to M2.

**Budget verdict:**
- **Potentially executable near RMB 100k self-funded** if low-cost student housing is secured and living is controlled.
- **Comfortably executable if the EUR 10k scholarship is won.**
- Housing is the main financial risk; private-market Paris-region housing can break the budget.

**Current strategic rank: #1 overall.**

### 2. University of Osaka — #95 — CORE TARGET / BEST SELF-FUNDED COST PREDICTABILITY

**Recommended route:**
- Graduate School of Engineering Science, Engineering Science 21st Century English programme;
- Systems Innovation / intelligent systems / robotics-aligned laboratory selection;
- monitor the MEXT-granted Robotics special programme if it continues in the user's application year.

**Why it fits:**
- The general Engineering Science 21st Century programme can be completed entirely in English.
- The current Systems Science / Systems Innovation environment explicitly spans information, control, intelligent systems and related engineering fields.
- Osaka currently also runs a MEXT-granted English Robotics programme, demonstrating strong institutional robotics depth, although that specific grant must not be assumed to continue in 2028.

**Current cost architecture:**
- Standard graduate tuition: JPY 535,800/year.
- Enrollment fee: JPY 282,000 one time.
- Osaka offers a full-tuition exemption for eligible excellent privately-funded international graduate students, and separate need-based exemption mechanisms.
- Current MEXT-granted Robotics scholarship provides tuition/entrance/exam exemption plus JPY 147,000/month for Master's students, but only a very small number of places and future continuation is uncertain.
- Official Study in Japan 2025–2026 data gives Kinki-region international-student living expenses around JPY 89,000/month excluding tuition (national average JPY 105,000/month).

**Budget verdict:**
- **Baseline first year is plausibly within roughly RMB 90–100k at current exchange rates** using Kinki-region living averages, even before a tuition waiver, with one-time admission fee concentrated in Year 1.
- Tuition waiver/MEXT makes it substantially cheaper.

**Current strategic rank: #2 overall; best predictable self-funded anchor.**

### 3. KAIST — #65 — CORE FUNDED REACH

**Recommended route:**
- School of Electrical Engineering with Robotics & Control research, or KAIST Robotics Program if admissions structure remains suitable.

**Why it fits:**
- KAIST EE explicitly has Robotics & Control research spanning control theory, autonomous vehicles, human-robot interaction, drones/UAM, humanoid and legged robots, distributed/data-driven control and related systems.
- The Robotics Program admits Master's students and is a direct robotics route.
- This is one of the cleanest matches to an electronics/information/control/software profile.

**Current funding architecture:**
- KAIST's current international graduate scholarship covers full tuition, National Health Insurance and a monthly stipend.
- KAIST states that 94% of its international student body benefits from this scholarship architecture.
- KGPS for highly competitive Master's candidates provides full tuition + KRW 1,000,000/month for four regular semesters.
- KAIST EE currently states minimum support for international MS students at KRW 1,000,000/month including health insurance, subject to the relevant scholarship/advisor structure.

**Budget verdict:**
- **With KAIST Scholarship: strongly within the RMB100k/year family-contribution constraint; potentially close to financially self-sustaining.**
- Self-funded KAIST is not budget-compliant and should not be treated as the executable route.

**Current strategic rank: #3 overall; highest-value funded target.**

### 4. Politecnico di Milano — #=87 — CORE CONDITIONAL TARGET

**Recommended programme:** M.Sc. Automation and Control Engineering (English).

**Why it fits:**
- Directly integrates mechanics, electronics, computing and control for autonomous systems.
- The current programme explicitly frames its direction as Physical AI and includes robotics, autonomous vehicles, perception, localization, planning, control, real-time systems, sensing and hardware/software integration.
- This is one of the most directionally precise programmes for the user's robotics / embodied-AI goal.

**Current cost architecture:**
- Non-EU students with a foreign Bachelor's and no scholarship normally pay the maximum tuition contribution; current maximum for a standard load is EUR 3,943.04/year.
- Polimi merit scholarships include full tuition waiver; Platinum/Gold awards currently also provide EUR 10,000 / EUR 8,000 gross allowances for selected students.
- Current Polimi residence room prices are commonly several thousand euros per academic year; Milan living remains materially more expensive than Osaka/Kyoto.

**Budget verdict:**
- **Without scholarship: not a safe RMB100k all-in choice.**
- **With full tuition waiver: potentially workable near the band if housing is controlled.**
- **With Gold/Platinum allowance: clearly financially executable.**

**Current strategic rank: #4; excellent fit, scholarship-dependent.**

### 5. Kyoto University — #64 — CORE BUDGET RESERVE

**Recommended route:** Graduate School of Informatics International Program, especially Intelligence Science and Technology, if final curricular admission fit is confirmed.

**Why it fits:**
- The International Program can be completed entirely in English.
- Intelligence Science has active robotics work including Symbol Emergence in Robotics, robot learning, multimodal intelligence, world models and embodied cognition.
- This is more AI/intelligent-robotics oriented than a classical EE/automation Master's, so the programme-level prerequisite audit matters more than for Paris-Saclay or Osaka.

**Current cost architecture:**
- Graduate tuition: JPY 535,800/year.
- Admission fee: JPY 282,000.
- Graduate students with strong academic records and financial difficulty may apply for full or half tuition exemption; competition is limited.
- Study in Japan currently estimates Kinki-region living expenses around JPY 89,000/month excluding tuition.

**Budget verdict:**
- **Baseline is plausibly near the RMB90–100k first-year band** under current exchange rates and moderate living.
- Tuition exemption improves margin.

**Current strategic rank: #5; strong affordable prestige reserve, but less curriculum-clean than Osaka/Paris-Saclay.**

### 6. Seoul National University — #38 — FUNDED REACH

**Recommended route:** Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering; Control Engineering / Intelligent Systems and Robotics / Physical AI / Robot Learning related labs.

**Why it fits:**
- SNU ECE explicitly maintains Control Engineering and Intelligent Systems & Robotics groups.
- Research includes robot learning, wearable robotics, vehicle intelligence, Physical AI, learning/decision systems, embedded systems and computer vision.
- Graduate curriculum includes Intelligent Robot and Applications, Optimal Control Theory and related systems/optimization work.

**Current cost architecture:**
- 2026 College of Engineering tuition: KRW 3,997,000 per semester.
- Current GSFS: full tuition up to four semesters + at least KRW 500,000/month, subject to selection and programme/college conditions.
- Current SNU Global Scholarship GS1 provides full tuition + KRW 1.2m/month + airfare, but the Spring 2026 call covers one semester/6 months rather than guaranteeing the full degree; GS2 covers full tuition for the relevant award period.

**Budget verdict:**
- **Self-funded: likely above the strict RMB100k all-in band.**
- **With reliable full tuition scholarship: workable; with stipend scholarship: strongly workable.**
- Funding continuity must be checked; do not assume a one-semester award covers two years.

**Current strategic rank: #6; high-quality funded reach.**

## Secondary / Reserve Pool

### University of Tokyo — #39

- International Multidisciplinary Engineering Graduate Program includes Electrical Engineering and Information Systems and is delivered in English.
- Current Master tuition JPY 535,800/year + JPY 282,000 admission + JPY 10,000 examination in the IME route.
- Top September applicants may receive MEXT or equivalent IME scholarships.
- Tokyo living cost is materially higher than Kinki; current JASSO Tokyo average is around JPY 130,000/month excluding tuition.
- **Verdict:** borderline over RMB100k in a conservative self-funded first year; strong if dorm/fee exemption/MEXT works. Keep as funded/lean-budget prestige reach.

### Institute of Science Tokyo — #=97

- Strong Systems and Control Engineering graduate major; current courses include system identification, optimal control, nonlinear/adaptive control, network control and planning algorithms, with English offerings.
- International Graduate Programs provide English-based degree paths; current ADB-JSP/MEXT-type routes can include tuition/stipend support for eligible candidates.
- Current annual tuition JPY 635,400 + JPY 282,000 enrollment; Tokyo living costs push conservative self-funded total above the user's band.
- **Verdict:** academically excellent and highly aligned, but funding/waiver is needed for budget safety.

### KTH Royal Institute of Technology — #=82

- MSc Systems, Control and Robotics is a direct match with Robotics & Autonomous Systems and Learning, Decision & Control tracks; English.
- Current tuition is SEK 360,000 for the full two-year programme.
- KTH Scholarship covers tuition but not living; only 8% of admitted scholarship applicants were nominated in 2025.
- Stockholm official current living estimate SEK 9,450–12,350/month.
- **Verdict:** even with tuition waiver, living alone consumes most or all of the RMB100k band. Lower-priority scholarship reserve.

### Technical University of Munich — #25

- ECE -> Automation and Robotics remains one of the strongest curriculum fits.
- Current third-country tuition for ECE is EUR 6,000/semester.
- Munich current living guidance is roughly EUR 1,250–1,300/month average; TUM states EUR 1,300–2,000/month for a single household as a broader planning range.
- **Verdict:** fails the current all-in budget by a wide margin. Even a tuition waiver does not reliably bring Munich living below the RMB100k band. Keep only as a major-funding / living-stipend moonshot.

## Key Strategic Conclusions

1. **Germany is no longer the obvious center of the target pool under the combined Top-100 + RMB100k/year hard filters.** TUM survives ranking but fails baseline affordability; RWTH and KIT fail the current ranking cutoff.
2. **Japan currently has the strongest predictable self-funded economics among Top-100 engineering universities** because national-university tuition is comparatively low and Osaka/Kyoto living costs are materially below Tokyo/Munich.
3. **Paris-Saclay currently offers an unusually strong Europe combination:** Top-100 rank, relevant English ECE/autonomous-systems pathways, low national registration fee under the present non-EU partial-exemption policy, low-cost CROUS housing possibilities and a substantial international scholarship.
4. **KAIST is the strongest scholarship-architecture target:** robotics/control fit is excellent and international graduate funding is unusually broad, but admission/funding remains competitive.
5. **Polimi is perhaps the most directionally explicit programme match** through Automation & Control / Physical AI, but Milan living + ordinary non-EU tuition makes merit scholarship materially important.
6. The correct application strategy should mix **self-funded-safe anchors + funded reaches**, rather than building a list where every offer is unusable without winning an exceptional scholarship.
