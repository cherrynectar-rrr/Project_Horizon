# MSc Application Gates Master Map — TUM-Primary, Multi-School

Last Updated: 2026-08-25
Owner: Voyage Room
Status: Strategic master map; advisory only

## Purpose

Maintain one complete map of the examinations, credential-verification gates, programme-specific screenings, application-material gates and post-offer administrative gates that may affect the user's 2029 overseas MSc application cycle.

The purpose is omission prevention. A branch being listed here does **not** mean it is active now. Project Control owns activation and workload sequencing.

Planning baseline:

- normal four-year undergraduate completion in 2029;
- TUM M.Sc. Electrical and Computer Engineering -> Automation and Robotics remains the primary benchmark;
- broader QS Top-100 / financially executable robotics-control-intelligent-systems pool remains open;
- TOEFL is the preferred common English examination;
- do not open simultaneous high-intensity German + TOEFL + GRE preparation.

## Master Branch Register

### G1 — German / TestDaF or telc C1 Hochschule

**Role:** Primary TUM ECE language gate.

Current 2026 TUM ECE requires German proof by the application deadline and is mainly German-taught.

Preferred current proof routes:

- TestDaF: TDN 4 in all four sections; or
- telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule: pass.

Planning ability target: genuine university-level C1 rather than certificate-only cramming.

Timing logic:

- TestDaF certificate is currently valid indefinitely, although a university may impose its own recency requirement.
- Therefore German can be solved early without the same expiry problem as TOEFL.

Horizon target:

- 2026–2027: A1/A2 -> B1 foundation;
- 2027–2028: B1 -> B2;
- 2028: B2 -> C1 / TestDaF-specific preparation;
- first serious official attempt during 2028 when mock results approach TDN4;
- target pass by end of 2028;
- early 2029 is an emergency retake window, not the intended first attempt.

Status: **Future mandatory if TUM ECE remains primary. Long runway; should be the earliest language line.**

---

### G2 — TOEFL iBT

**Role:** Preferred common English test across the broader retained pool.

Current retained routes with TOEFL use/acceptance include KAIST, KTH, Osaka Engineering Science, University of Tokyo routes, Polimi and other English-track programmes; programme-specific thresholds must be rechecked in the actual application cycle.

Current Horizon planning target under the post-2026 TOEFL scale:

- overall around 5.0 or higher;
- balanced sections;
- this is a planning margin target, not a universal minimum.

Timing logic:

- ETS currently keeps TOEFL scores valid for only 2 years.
- Therefore an official TOEFL attempt should not be taken unnecessarily early for a 2028–2029 application season.

Horizon target:

- build English continuously before the test;
- first official TOEFL preferably in 2028, early enough to allow a retake before autumn/winter 2028 deadlines;
- have a valid usable TOEFL score before the earliest application deadlines, not merely before TUM's May 2029 deadline.

Status: **Broad-pool mandatory / highly reusable. Do not duplicate with IELTS unless a later programme creates a genuine incompatibility.**

---

### G3 — GRE General

**Role:** Conditional standardized-test branch.

Current 2026 examples:

- TUM ECE: no GRE requirement.
- TUM Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence (RCI): applicants with a degree from China currently need GRE General with Quantitative >=164 and Analytical Writing >=4.0; no current Verbal minimum.
- Selected University of Tokyo routes have used GRE General in current/recent admissions.
- Polimi may treat GRE as optional rather than universally required.

Current Horizon target if activated:

- Quantitative 166+;
- Analytical Writing 4.0+;
- Verbal 150+ as a planning competitiveness target, not a TUM formal minimum.

Timing logic:

- ETS currently makes GRE scores reportable for 5 years.
- This gives more timing flexibility than TOEFL.

Activation gate:

- do **not** prepare GRE now;
- target pool must be narrowed no later than 2028 H1;
- activate only if RCI / a GRE-requiring University of Tokyo route / another sufficiently valuable programme remains in the real application pool.

Status: **Conditional. Preserve the option; do not consume current execution capacity.**

---

### G4 — APS Academic Evaluation / TestAS or Interview Route

**Role:** Germany-specific Chinese-credential verification gate; not merely a language exam.

Current 2026 APS pilot rules show that undergraduate students in the general domestic applicant procedure may use TestAS or interview depending the then-current procedure. APS explicitly recommends that students may complete the general procedure before graduation and later submit the final bachelor's degree and graduation certificates for re-verification without another interview. The 2026 TestAS arrangement is explicitly a pilot and must not be hard-coded for 2028–2029.

Important current facts:

- APS certificate is currently used for German university applications / visa processes for affected Chinese applicants;
- current TUM international-application pages identify APS as a special document requirement for Chinese credentials in relevant programmes;
- exact TestAS vs interview procedure and timing can change.

Horizon target:

- recheck APS procedure in 2027 and again in 2028;
- aim to complete the usable APS gate during 2028 if the then-current rules allow and Germany remains in the final pool;
- do not wait until the TUM application deadline to discover the procedure.

Status: **Germany mandatory gate under current rules; procedure is time-sensitive and must be reverified.**

---

### G5 — uni-assist VPD for TUM / German Applications

**Role:** Credential pre-evaluation, not an exam.

Current TUM ECE requires applicants with foreign university entrance qualifications to obtain preliminary documentation (VPD) through uni-assist. TUM states processing may take up to about four weeks and recommends starting early.

Horizon target:

- determine exact 2029 VPD + APS document order during 2028 application-preparation review;
- do not place the VPD request in the final days before TUM's application deadline;
- by early 2029, all Germany-specific credential documents should be in a ready state.

Status: **Mandatory for TUM under current rules. Administrative, but omission can invalidate an otherwise strong application.**

---

### G6 — TUM ECE Aptitude Assessment / Possible Interview

**Role:** Programme-specific admission screening.

Current 2026 structure:

- Stage 1 evaluates curricular qualification + grade;
- >=75 points: direct Stage-1 pass;
- 60–74: interview;
- <60: fail Stage 1;
- current interview is usually online and approximately 20 minutes.

Preparation should come primarily from undergraduate coursework, GPA, preserved syllabi and technical communication rather than a separate early cram line.

Horizon target:

- keep core-course evidence and English/German technical explanation ability throughout undergraduate study;
- run formal TUM score simulation after Year 2, after Year 3, and before application;
- if Stage-1 simulation remains near interview range, conduct targeted mock interviews in 2029 after application submission.

Status: **Possible conditional screening even when all documents are complete.**

---

### G7 — Programme-Specific Oral / Written / Internet Screening

**Role:** Catch-all branch for non-TUM programmes whose current admissions involve oral, written or internet screening.

Current examples:

- University of Osaka Engineering Science 21st Century overseas route currently uses application-document screening plus internet interview; domestic variants may use oral/paper examinations.
- University of Tokyo graduate engineering routes may have department-specific entrance examinations and externally submitted TOEFL scores.
- Kyoto / Science Tokyo / other Japanese routes can have programme-specific interviews, presentations or examinations depending the exact route.
- Some Korean programmes may use department-level interviews even when the central international application has no standardized technical exam.

Horizon rule:

- do not create one generic 'Japan exam' curriculum now;
- once the real target programmes are frozen in 2028, build a programme-by-programme screening sheet with exact exam format, subjects, language, deadline and past-paper availability.

Status: **Conditional by programme. Must be audited before each application, not assumed away.**

---

### A1 — Application Calendar / Deadline Control

**Role:** Mandatory omission-prevention branch.

This is not an exam, but it controls all exams and documents.

Critical fact: the application season begins earlier than TUM's April–May window. For example, KTH's current 2027-entry cycle opens in October of the previous year and closes in January. Therefore a 2029-entry portfolio may begin submitting applications in autumn 2028.

Horizon target:

- target-pool freeze v1: no later than 2028 H1;
- create one line per programme containing application open, deadline, score-send deadline, scholarship deadline, recommendation deadline, interview window and result date;
- treat the **earliest** school deadline as the deadline that controls reusable test timing.

Status: **Mandatory. This is the master control branch for the application season.**

---

### A2 — Academic Record / Course-Evidence Package

**Role:** Mandatory academic gate, especially important for TUM curricular analysis.

Maintain:

- official transcript;
- GPA / grading-scale documentation;
- class rank or rank certificate if available;
- official course syllabi / module descriptions;
- credits and contact hours;
- key laboratory / course-design evidence;
- English translations where required;
- later, a clean course-guide package for key mathematics, physics, ECE, control, signal, embedded and software courses.

This branch is continuous because missing historical syllabi can be difficult to reconstruct in the application year.

Status: **Mandatory and active as evidence preservation, but low workload.**

---

### A3 — CV / Motivation / Recommendations / Research-Fit Materials

**Role:** Common application-material branch.

Depending on programme, may include:

- CV;
- statement / motivation letter;
- research proposal or research-interest statement;
- recommendation letters;
- professor / laboratory contact;
- project portfolio / publications / certificates;
- programme-specific summary sheets.

Current examples:

- KTH Systems, Control and Robotics requires CV, motivation letter, two recommendation letters and a programme summary sheet.
- Paris-Saclay's current M1 International Track requires motivation letter, transcripts, English-level certificate and CV.
- Osaka's English programme currently requests recommendation material in addition to language proof and interview screening.

Horizon target:

- build evidence now through real work;
- identify recommenders during 2027–2028 rather than asking strangers at the last minute;
- draft reusable core materials in 2028 H2, then customize per programme.

Status: **Mandatory across the pool, but programme-specific details vary.**

---

### A4 — Scholarship / Funding Application Branch

**Role:** Financial executability gate, especially for TUM, KAIST, SNU, Polimi, Tokyo and other funding-dependent options.

This branch is separate because scholarship deadlines and required documents may differ from programme admission deadlines.

Possible items include:

- university merit scholarships;
- MEXT / programme-granted Japanese funding;
- KAIST scholarship architecture;
- SNU GSFS / Global Scholarship;
- Polimi merit scholarships;
- Paris-Saclay International Master's Scholarship;
- TUM waivers / scholarships and other reliable funding routes.

Horizon rule:

- no offer counts as financially executable merely because admission is possible;
- track scholarship deadlines separately from admission deadlines;
- verify current funding continuity and conditions in the real cycle.

Status: **Mandatory for funding-dependent targets; strategic importance high.**

---

### P1 — Post-Offer Visa / Enrollment / Proof-of-Funds Branch

**Role:** Post-admission execution; not an admission exam but capable of blocking actual enrollment.

Typical categories:

- student visa / residence application;
- proof of funds / blocked-account or country-specific equivalent;
- health insurance;
- certified / notarized final documents;
- final bachelor's diploma / degree certificate;
- final APS re-verification where then-current German procedure requires it;
- tuition / semester contribution;
- enrollment deadlines;
- housing / relocation.

Status: **Deferred until offers, but should have an outline before application because funding and visa feasibility affect school choice.**

## Safety-First Timeline

### 2026 H2 — Foundation / No Application Panic

- German A1 -> A2 begins as the longest-runway language.
- English ability continues, but no need to burn TOEFL validity now.
- No GRE activation.
- Preserve syllabi / transcript / grading-scale evidence.
- No assumption that 2026 APS TestAS pilot rules will remain unchanged.

### 2027 — Build Language and Evidence

- German A2 -> B1, potentially toward B2 depending sustainable pace.
- Build TOEFL skills without requiring an official score yet.
- Continue GPA/core-course evidence and project/research evidence.
- Review target pool once during the year for major rule/cost changes.
- Identify possible recommenders and laboratories, but do not mass-contact prematurely.

### 2028 H1 — Freeze the Real Application Architecture

This is the critical decision phase.

By approximately mid-2028:

- freeze Target Pool v1 for the 2029 cycle;
- identify the **earliest** application deadline among all retained schools;
- decide whether GRE remains necessary;
- recheck APS procedure and Germany documentation;
- move German from general B2/C1 development into TestDaF/telc preparation when ready;
- begin official TOEFL timing so the score remains valid throughout the whole application season.

### 2028 H2 — Main Exam + Application Preparation Season

Desired state by the end of 2028:

- TestDaF 4x4 / accepted German proof: ideally DONE;
- TOEFL: valid target-level score DONE;
- GRE: DONE only if the target pool activated it;
- APS: ideally DONE / usable under then-current rules if Germany remains active;
- programme-specific exam/interview sheets prepared;
- recommenders confirmed;
- CV / core motivation material / research-fit material ready;
- course-guide / transcript package organized;
- scholarship calendar ready;
- earliest applications may already be submitted from autumn 2028.

### 2029 Jan–Mar — Backup + Early Applications

- retake TOEFL / TestDaF only if needed;
- complete conditional GRE backup only if still justified and deadlines permit;
- submit / finish schools with winter deadlines;
- ensure APS/VPD/TUM document chain is ready;
- conduct programme-specific written/oral/interview preparation where invitations or schedules require it.

### 2029 Apr–May — TUM Main Application Window Under Current Rules

By this point there should be **no first-time language exam dependency**.

Current TUM ECE winter application window: 1 April–31 May.

Submit TUM application with:

- valid German proof;
- transcript / degree evidence allowed under then-current rule;
- VPD;
- required China-specific documents including APS as applicable;
- current German CV and all programme documents;
- complete curricular evidence.

### 2029 Jun–Sep — Interviews / Offers / Visa / Enrollment

- TUM aptitude interview if invited;
- other programme interviews / final screening;
- funding decision comparison;
- visa / proof-of-funds / insurance / enrollment;
- final degree / graduation-document supplementation;
- final route decision based on programme fit + funding + life/wealth implications.
