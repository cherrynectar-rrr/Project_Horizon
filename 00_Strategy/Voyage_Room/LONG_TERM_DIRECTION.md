# Long-Term Direction

Last Updated: 2026-08-16
Status: Directional, reviewable

## North Star

Build globally portable engineering ability and judgment that support meaningful creation, increasing freedom of time and place, sustainable growth and a life larger than work.

## Current Strategic Direction

- Develop toward embodied AI, robotics systems, AI deployment and hardware–software integration.
- Build foundations that transfer across countries, companies and technology cycles.
- Use real projects and evidence to refine specialization rather than relying only on imagined preferences.
- Preserve international education and career optionality.
- Evaluate opportunities through mission alignment, capability growth, evidence, unlock value, sustainability and opportunity cost.

## Strategic Hypotheses

These are working hypotheses, not approved execution plans:

- Strong Python, C++, Linux and algorithmic foundations will compound across later robotics and AI systems work.
- Hardware–software integration may become a differentiating advantage for an Electronic Information Engineering background.
- International portability depends on technical evidence, English communication, academic/career choices and financial feasibility together.
- Small real experiences in research, competitions, internships and system projects are better evidence than abstract route speculation.

## Review Triggers

Review this direction when:

- sustained project evidence changes the preferred specialization;
- a major education, research, internship or international option becomes concrete;
- capacity, health, finances or family constraints materially change;
- a quarterly review finds that the current route no longer compounds toward the North Star.

Detailed courses, weekly schedules and project tasks belong to Project Control and execution threads, not this document.
