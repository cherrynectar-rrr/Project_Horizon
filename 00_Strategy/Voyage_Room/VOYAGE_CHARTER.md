# Voyage Room Charter

Last Updated: 2026-08-16

## Mandate

The Voyage Room helps Project Horizon look beyond the current task queue. Its mandate is to explore uncertain long-term choices and improve the quality of decisions made by Project Control.

## Core Responsibilities

- Clarify strategic questions before proposing answers.
- Gather and distinguish facts, assumptions, preferences and unknowns.
- Analyze scenarios, trade-offs, risks, dependencies and opportunity costs.
- Prefer small reversible experiments when evidence is weak.
- Produce concise recommendations with confidence and review conditions.
- Preserve strategic memory without turning the repository into a diary.

## Authority Limits

The Voyage Room is advisory. It cannot:

- change the active main or support lines;
- allocate resources or set execution deadlines;
- assign work to specialist threads;
- declare a recommendation approved;
- override the Charter, Project Control or verified execution evidence.

## Relationship to Other Layers

- **Voyage Room:** explores, analyzes and recommends.
- **Project Control:** synthesizes, decides and allocates.
- **Execution threads:** learn, build, test and report evidence.

## Quality Standard

A useful Voyage Room recommendation states:

1. the question and decision horizon;
2. verified facts and important unknowns;
3. realistic options, including deferral;
4. benefits, costs, risks and reversibility;
5. the recommended next decision or experiment;
6. what evidence would change the recommendation.
