# MSc Application Exam Strategy — TUM-Primary, Multi-School Coverage

Last Updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Voyage Room
Status: Strategic recommendation; advisory only

## Purpose

Design the smallest reusable exam-preparation stack that preserves the current QS Top-100 affordable robotics/automation MSc pool while keeping TUM as the user's primary target/benchmark.

This document does not activate language/GRE execution by itself. Project Control owns workload allocation.

## User-Confirmed Position

- Preserve the broader target pool rather than collapsing to TUM only.
- TUM remains the primary target/benchmark.
- **Early graduation is no longer under consideration; use the normal four-year undergraduate timeline and 2029 bachelor's completion as the planning baseline.**
- Preparation should maximize cross-school reuse and avoid sitting multiple redundant language tests.
- **TOEFL is acceptable as the preferred common English examination instead of IELTS.**

## Recommended Minimum Exam Stack

### 1. English — one exam only

Preferred common exam: **TOEFL iBT**.

Planning target under the post-2026 TOEFL scale:

- **Overall 5.0 or higher** as the main Horizon target;
- this corresponds to the historical approximately-100/120 planning level used before the January-2026 scoring change;
- section balance matters; do not optimize only the total score while leaving one skill materially weak.

ETS changed TOEFL iBT scoring on 2026-01-21 to a 1–6 scale in 0.5 increments. During the transition through January 2028, score reports also display a comparable 0–120 overall score. ETS maps common historical requirements approximately as follows: 80 -> 4.0, 90 -> 4.5, 100 -> 5.0. A score of 5.0 is aligned with CEFR C1 on the current scale.

The 5.0 target is a Horizon margin target, not a claim that every programme requires it. Current programme minima are generally lower and vary by programme.

Current examples:

- TUM general English-language proof: TOEFL iBT historical-equivalent 88; TUM now also accepts the corresponding new TOEFL score. **The primary TUM ECE route itself is mainly German-taught and its main language gate is German, not TOEFL.**
- University of Osaka Engineering Science international Master's route: TOEFL is explicitly accepted as English proof.
- KAIST international graduate admission: TOEFL iBT 4.5 under the post-2026 scale (historically 83) or IELTS 6.5.
- Seoul National University international graduate admission: TOEFL iBT historical-equivalent 80 or IELTS Academic 6.0.
- KTH English 6-equivalent requirement: current published guidance uses approximately TOEFL 90 / IELTS 6.5 equivalents.
- Kyoto Informatics International Program: TOEFL/TOEIC/IELTS scores are accepted and can contribute to admissions assessment depending on the route.
- University of Tokyo routes under consideration accept or explicitly use TOEFL; some Japanese graduate programmes prefer or specifically structure score submission around TOEFL.
- Politecnico di Milano accepts TOEFL among recognized English certificates.
- Paris-Saclay M1 International Track in Electrical Engineering requires proof/certification of English level for non-English speakers; the programme page does not currently publish one universal TOEFL numerical minimum.

### Why TOEFL is a good common choice for the current pool

TOEFL has particularly strong reuse across the retained Japanese, Korean, Swedish, Italian and German-English-requirement routes. It therefore works well as the single standardized English exam for Horizon.

**Do not take both TOEFL and IELTS unless a later target programme creates a real incompatibility.**

### 2. German — primary TUM-specific long-horizon requirement

Primary TUM route: **M.Sc. Electrical and Computer Engineering -> Automation and Robotics**.

Current 2026 requirement: German proficiency must be proved by the application deadline; programme is mainly taught in German.

Preferred certificate route:

- **TestDaF: TDN 4 in all four sections**, or
- **telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule: pass**.

Horizon should treat the practical target as university-level B2-to-C1 competence, not certificate-only cramming.

Because TUM is the primary target and German takes much longer to build than GRE, German is the unique requirement that deserves the earliest strategic runway if Project Control approves language activation.

### 3. GRE General — preserve RCI / selected high-upside routes, but prepare later

GRE is **not currently required for TUM ECE**.

It is currently required/relevant for:

- TUM Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence (RCI) for applicants whose first degree is from China: Quantitative 164 minimum, Analytical Writing 4.0 minimum, no Verbal minimum under current rules;
- selected University of Tokyo international engineering routes such as IME under current rules;
- Politecnico di Milano: optional and potentially useful, but not a universal requirement.

Recommended Horizon planning target if GRE remains necessary in the final pool:

- Quantitative: **166+**
- Analytical Writing: **4.0+**
- Verbal: **150+** as a sensible competitiveness target, not a TUM RCI formal minimum.

GRE should be prepared after English is sufficiently stable and only if RCI / UTokyo or another GRE-requiring target remains alive close enough to the application cycle.

## Programme-Specific Oral / Technical Screening

This is not one standardized exam, but a common technical interview package can cover several schools.

Current examples include TUM aptitude interviews in some cases, University of Osaka internet interviews, Kyoto presentation/Q&A routes, and possible University of Tokyo / Science Tokyo interviews or examinations.

Common technical-interview preparation base:

- higher mathematics: calculus, linear algebra, probability;
- circuits / analog / digital electronics fundamentals;
- signals and systems + DSP;
- control fundamentals;
- algorithms and data structures;
- C/C++ / Python and project implementation;
- embedded systems / sensors when relevant;
- ability to explain bachelor courses from the transcript;
- research interests, research proposal and project evidence;
- ability to discuss the above clearly in English.

This should be built through the normal undergraduate/Horizon capability route rather than as a separate exam-cramming curriculum.

## Germany Administrative Gate — APS

APS is not a normal university admission test but remains a Germany-specific gate for applicants with Chinese university credentials.

Current 2026 China procedure requires credential verification plus the applicable APS / TestAS process under then-current rules. Because procedures have changed recently, Horizon must re-check the actual rule before the application cycle rather than committing to a fixed 2026 workflow now.

With early graduation removed, the planning baseline is the normal 2029 bachelor's timeline, which reduces unnecessary APS/application compression.

## Languages Not Worth Activating for Admission Right Now

For the currently selected English-track routes, there is no present need to add entrance-exam preparation for:

- Japanese;
- Korean;
- French;
- Italian.

These languages may later have strong life/internship/employment value, but they should not be opened now merely for MSc admission while TUM remains the main target.

## Current Priority Order — Strategic, Not Yet an Execution Schedule

1. **German** — longest runway; uniquely important because TUM is primary.
2. **English / TOEFL iBT** — universal application infrastructure across almost the entire pool.
3. **Technical foundations + interview communication** — built through GPA/coursework/projects rather than a separate test track.
4. **GRE General** — later, conditional on RCI / UTokyo / another GRE target remaining in the final pool.
5. **APS preparation** — Germany-specific and timed to the actual application/APS cycle.

## Important Guardrail

Do not translate this stack into simultaneous high-intensity German + TOEFL + GRE preparation during the current undergraduate stage.

The correct principle is:

> preserve all required future exits now; activate each standardized test only when its lead time and application deadline justify the opportunity cost.

## Recheck Triggers

- Target pool is narrowed in 2027–2028.

- APS changes China-procedure requirements.
