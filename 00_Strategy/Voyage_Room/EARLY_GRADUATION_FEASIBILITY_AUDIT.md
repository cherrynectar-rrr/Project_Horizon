# Three-Year Early Graduation Feasibility Audit — Electronic Information Engineering

Last Updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Voyage Room
Status: Strategic feasibility audit; advisory only

## Question

Can the user complete Qingdao Agricultural University's Electronic Information Engineering bachelor's degree in three years (2025–2028), while preserving the stronger long-term route toward robotics / intelligent systems and overseas MSc applications?

## Executive Verdict

**Conditionally feasible, but not yet executable.**

The university's current credit-system rules explicitly permit four-year undergraduates to apply for early graduation, and the user's curriculum itself specifies a 3–8 year permitted study period. The credit threshold for applying can plausibly be reached by the start of Year 3.

However, the decisive bottleneck is **not raw credit arithmetic**. It is whether the college will formally advance or reschedule the normally Semester-7/8 professional practice, comprehensive internship, graduation internship and thesis, and whether key Semester-7 electives can be taken early when strategically necessary.

A second strategic issue is overseas-MSc timing: graduating in summer 2028 does not automatically mean the user can start a German MSc in autumn 2028. APS/VPD/application timing and the availability of enough completed transcript evidence must be rechecked in the 2027–2028 cycle.

Voyage Room therefore recommends preserving three-year graduation as a **real option under investigation**, not committing to it yet.

## 1. Verified University Rule

Current official rule: `青岛农业大学学分制管理办法` (青农大校字〔2025〕151号; issued 2025-12-29).

For an undergraduate to apply for early graduation, the current rule requires:

- moral/intellectual/physical/aesthetic/labor requirements qualified;
- at least 60% of the curriculum credits already obtained;
- average GPA above the stated 3.0 threshold (the official sentence is redundantly worded as `大于3.0及以上`; plan conservatively for >3.0);
- no failed courses;
- application in the first week of the fall semester;
- graduation may be advanced only by whole academic years, not half-years;
- after college approval and Academic Affairs approval, the student may enter graduation internship and thesis teaching arrangements.

For a four-year-or-longer bachelor's degree, current permitted study duration is 3–8 years.

Official source:
- https://jw.qau.edu.cn/userfiles/files/jwc/2025/12/20251230103214.pdf

The user's own Electronic Information Engineering curriculum independently states:
- standard duration: 4 years;
- permitted duration: 3–8 years;
- total graduation credits: 170.

Therefore the earliest structurally possible graduation is after 3 academic years.

## 2. Application Threshold at the Start of Semester 5

For this user, 60% of 170 credits = **102 credits**.

A three-year route implies:

- entry: September 2025;
- finish Semester 4: summer 2027;
- submit early-graduation application: first week of fall 2027 (start of Semester 5);
- targeted graduation: summer 2028 (end of Semester 6).

### Conservative estimate by the end of Semester 4

Using the official curriculum and only credits that can reasonably be expected to have completed / posted by then:

| Category | Estimated completed credits by end of S4 |
| --- | ---: |
| Required classroom courses S1–S4 | 79.5 |
| Clearly completed practice components by S4 | ~9 |
| Planned general electives S3–S4 | 4 |
| User-confirmed professional electives in S3: Linux 2 + MATLAB 3 + Data Structures 3 | 8 |
| **Estimated total** | **~100.5** |

This is approximately **1.5 credits below** the 102-credit application threshold under a conservative posting assumption.

A clean way to create margin is to take **Python Language Programming (3 credits, normally S4)**. Under the same assumptions, the estimated end-S4 total rises to ~103.5 credits.

Important: the application threshold must be checked against the **actual posted transcript/credit record**, not this planning estimate. Long-span courses such as Labor Education, Physical Health Test, Second Classroom and Research Training may post credits differently.

## 4. General Elective Compression

The curriculum requires 10 general-elective credits and normally distributes 2 credits in each of S3–S7.

A three-year student has only S3–S6, so the full 10 credits must be completed by the end of S6. If 4 credits are completed in S3–S4 as planned, the remaining 6 credits must be taken across S5–S6, meaning at least one of those semesters must carry 4 general-elective credits instead of the normal 2.

The user must still satisfy the category minima (e.g. aesthetics, Four Histories, safety education, and the required new-generation information-technology item for the 2025 cohort).

This is administratively plausible, but actual selection limits / course availability must be checked in the live system.

## 5. The Real Bottleneck — Practice, Internship and Thesis

The correct Electronic Information Engineering curriculum places major late-stage components as follows:

- S7: Signal Processing and Transmission Comprehensive Practice — 1 credit
- S7: Professional Comprehensive Practice — 5 credits
- S8: Graduation Practice — 4 credits
- S8: Graduation Thesis (Design) — 6 credits
- Research Training and Course Paper — 2 credits, officially spanning S3–S7

These late-stage components represent at least **16 credits** that normally occur after Semester 6, before even considering how the research-training credit is posted.

The current early-graduation rule explicitly says that, after approval, an early-graduation applicant may enter graduation internship and thesis teaching arrangements. This strongly suggests an administrative path exists for advancing at least those elements.

But the published rule does **not** specify:

- how the S7 5-credit professional comprehensive practice is moved;
- whether the S7 signal-processing practice can be advanced;
- whether the thesis can formally start in S5 or S6 and how its required duration is scheduled;
- whether practice blocks can be completed during winter/summer breaks;
- whether the college has semester-load or sequence constraints for early graduates.

Therefore **college implementation is the decisive gate**.

A paper schedule that ignores these practice blocks is not a valid feasibility proof.

## 6. Workload Reality

If the user reaches approximately 103.5 posted credits at the start of S5, roughly 66.5 credits remain to reach 170 by the end of S6.

Not all of those are ordinary classroom credits; many are concentrated practices and thesis credits. Even so, the third academic year becomes a compressed combination of:

- remaining core course(s);
- professional electives;
- remaining general electives;
- multiple mandatory professional practices;
- professional comprehensive practice;
- graduation internship;
- thesis / design;
- graduate-school preparation if applying immediately abroad.

This is a **high-load configuration**. The main risk is not merely fatigue; it is deterioration of GPA, project/research depth, recommendation quality and MSc prerequisite coverage.

For Horizon, the relevant question is not `Can 170 be packed into 3 years?` but:

> Does finishing one year earlier create more long-term value than the evidence and optionality sacrificed to compress the final year?

## 7. Germany / TUM Timing Implication

Current 2026 TUM rules show that some master's programmes permit application before the bachelor's degree is fully completed. For example, TUM Electrical and Computer Engineering currently accepts either final graduation documents or a sufficiently advanced transcript; TUM RCI requests an official transcript of studies to date plus curriculum/module descriptions. Application windows for winter entry close around late May under current rules.

This means summer-2028 graduation could, in principle, be paired with a winter-2028/29 MSc application **if all external administrative requirements line up**.

However, there are several serious timing uncertainties:

1. **APS timing.** Current APS guidance recommends a four-year bachelor's student who plans to leave immediately after graduation to apply after completing Semester 6. Current temporary TestAS procedures create alternative routes, but those procedures are changing. A three-year graduate trying to start a German MSc immediately in autumn 2028 must not assume APS will fit the application deadline without written confirmation in the 2027–2028 cycle.
2. **VPD / uni-assist timing.** TUM international applications commonly require VPD; it must be available by the programme's deadline under then-current rules.
3. **Transcript maturity.** Courses taken only in S6 may not have final grades available during February–May applications. If a target MSc needs a specific formal prerequisite, it is safer to complete it by the end of S5 when possible.
4. **Credit equivalence.** Do not equate Chinese local credits directly with TUM/ECTS application thresholds. The relevant programme and uni-assist/TUM conversion rules must be used.
5. **RCI-specific evidence.** Current TUM RCI rules have programme-specific control and GRE requirements; these must be rechecked in 2028.

Therefore `graduate one year early` and `start Germany one year early` are **two different gates**.

## 8. Strategic Upside

If operationally feasible without degrading the application profile, early graduation could:

- save roughly one year of undergraduate time and related living / opportunity cost;
- move the international transition forward by up to one year;
- create an extra year for MSc, paid work, research or capital formation;
- reduce time spent in low-value institutional coursework if the remaining year would not otherwise produce strong evidence.

If immediate 2028 MSc entry is not feasible, a three-year graduation could still create a 2028–2029 gap year for strong research, internship / engineering work, language / application preparation or income generation. That scenario must be compared against simply using the fourth university year for the same goals while retaining student status and easier access to faculty / internships.

---

## 9. Strategic Downside

The one-year compression can damage exactly the factors that strong robotics / ECE MSc applications value:

- GPA / ranking;
- complete prerequisite coverage;
- research experience and recommendation letters;
- meaningful internship windows;
- deep engineering projects rather than shallow course completion;
- thesis quality;
- English / German / GRE / APS preparation;
- health and sustainable execution.

The current school threshold (>3.0 GPA) is only an **administrative early-graduation minimum**. It should never become Horizon's academic target. For a strong international MSc route, maintaining a strong transcript matters more than merely qualifying to graduate early.

## 10. Five Questions That Decide Whether This Option Is Real

Before any workload is increased, ask the Electronic Information Engineering college / teaching secretary in writing or in a way that can be reliably recorded:

1. For a 2025-cohort EIE student approved for three-year graduation at the start of S5, **how are S7/S8 mandatory practice, professional comprehensive practice, graduation internship and thesis formally rescheduled into S5/S6?**
2. Can an approved early-graduation student take a normally-S7 professional elective with the senior cohort in S5, specifically **Computer Control Technology**? What approval / registration process is required?
3. Can `Research Training and Course Paper`, `Second Classroom`, Labor Education and other long-span credits be completed and **posted before the relevant early-graduation audit dates**?
4. Are there any per-semester credit, limited-elective, timetable, prerequisite or practice-sequence caps specifically affecting early-graduation students?
5. What is the exact graduation / degree audit date for a three-year student, and must all 170 credits and all module minima be posted before that audit?

If the answer to Question 1 is negative or operationally unworkable, the three-year route should be dropped immediately; no need to sacrifice GPA trying to force it.

## 11. Voyage Room Recommendation

**Status: OPTION — investigate, do not commit yet.**

Recommended sequence:

1. Preserve current Year-2 academic / Horizon priorities; do not overload now merely because early graduation is theoretically possible.
2. Obtain authoritative answers to the five college-implementation questions.
3. If the practice/thesis rescheduling path is real, verify end-S4 posted-credit math and ensure >102 credits with margin, preferably using strategically useful S4 coursework rather than filler credits.
4. By 2027, run a separate `2028 Germany Immediate-Entry Audit` covering APS, VPD, TUM/target-project deadlines, transcript maturity, prerequisites, language/GRE and funding.
5. Only then ask Project Control to choose between:
   - normal 2029 graduation;
   - 2028 early graduation + immediate MSc;
   - 2028 early graduation + deliberate gap year.

### Current confidence

- High: university policy permits three-year completion in principle.
- High: 102-credit application threshold is numerically reachable by start of S5.
- Medium: professional elective requirements can be compressed to S3–S6 while preserving reasonable robotics alignment.
- Low-to-medium: college will permit all required S7/S8 practice/thesis rescheduling; not publicly documented in enough detail.
- Low-to-medium: 2028 Germany application administration will support immediate same-year transition; APS/VPD/project rules are time-sensitive and must be re-verified.
