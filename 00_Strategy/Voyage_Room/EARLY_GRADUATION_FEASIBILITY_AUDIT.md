## Active Planning Consequences

The normal four-year timeline gives Horizon more room for:

- GPA and class-rank protection;
- complete formal prerequisite coverage;
- control / signals / embedded / sensing / perception coursework;
- meaningful engineering projects;
- internships and research exposure;
- stronger thesis and recommendation evidence;
- German and English preparation;
- GRE only if later target programmes still justify it;

This does not imply that four years must be filled with low-value activity. The fourth year should still be judged by opportunity cost and used for high-value evidence, applications, research, internships, thesis quality and transition preparation.
