# Personal Finance & Capital Status

Last Updated: 2026-08-24
Status: Active — Bounded Support / Maintenance

## Primary Responsibility

Build and maintain a safe, evidence-based personal-finance operating system covering cash flow, budgeting, liquidity, investable-money boundaries, investment literacy, and later asset allocation as the user's real life stage changes.

This thread does **not** own academic, graduate-school, country, career, or cross-thread priority decisions.

## Current Goal

Complete **Personal Finance Baseline v1**.

The baseline should establish, at an appropriate level of detail:

1. recurring monthly cash inflow / support;
2. realistic essential spending floor;
3. discretionary, growth-related, and irregular spending categories;
4. liquid reserve / safety-buffer target;
5. the boundary between money that must remain liquid and money that is genuinely investable;
6. current asset categories at a non-sensitive summary level;
7. a lightweight monthly review process.

## Current Stage

The Finance thread is formally active as **bounded support / maintenance**, not as a second main line.

Undergraduate-stage execution should remain deliberately low-time and focus on:

- cash-flow visibility and sustainable budgeting;
- a realistic spending floor rather than deprivation for its own sake;
- reserve / liquidity discipline;
- separation of tuition, essential living, and emergency money from investable money;
- understanding diversification, volatility, fees, liquidity, and basic fund / portfolio structure;
- avoiding FOMO, frequent-trading habits, leverage, and ruin-level risk;
- protecting GPA, technical development, health, and future mobility from finance-related distraction.

## Authorized Work

- monthly budget and expense review;
- reserve / liquidity target maintenance;
- investment-literacy tasks;
- portfolio / fund structure review at an appropriate level of detail;
- later asset-allocation and Investment Policy Statement maintenance when justified by real capital and life stage;
- event-driven reviews when income, expenses, study-abroad costs, internship salary, tax residency, currency exposure, or major investment decisions materially change;
- report any finance decision that materially affects Horizon priorities to Project Control.

## Not Authorized

- independent changes to academic, career, country, graduate-school, or technical priorities;
- high-frequency trading as a default roadmap;
- leverage or speculative use of tuition, essential living money, or emergency reserves;
- guaranteed-return assumptions;
- finance work that materially displaces Python, Algorithm, GPA/core coursework, or approved Career / Internship work without Project Control review.

## Operating Cadence

Default workload ceiling:

- one initial baseline session;
- one short monthly review;
- event-driven work only when a material financial change or decision occurs.

No standing market-watching quota is authorized.

## Privacy / Security Boundary

Do not store passwords, authentication codes, full account numbers, bank-card details, brokerage credentials, or other sensitive secrets in GitHub.
